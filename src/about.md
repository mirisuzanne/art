title: (Terrible) Mia
sub: a portrait of Miriam Suzanne around middle-age
---

While some people change careers along the way
(and I still have time)
I've spent the last 20-some years
pursuing _simultaneous_ careers:
theater with [Grapefruit Lab](#),
music with [Teacup Gorilla](#),
web design with [OddBird](#),
and several other freelance arts on my own --
from digital prints to fiber crafts and pottery.

I enjoy bringing my perspective
to a creative process.
Not creative as in _magical_ or _unique_,
but _an act of creation_.
I enjoy looking at a mug,
and having opinions about it's shape --
or questions about the texture --
and then exploring my curiosity
in the process of making another mug.

I enjoy learning skills
that I can use to shape things around me.
I'm interested in the ways
a process and a material push back,
forcing me to adjust and reconsider.
I have no interest in a world where
I can conjure up exactly what's in my head.
Why bother?
I'm constantly imagining the wrong things.

I enjoy sharing the results with other people --
both the things I've made,
and the things I've learned.
I love imagining ways to communicate an idea,
and I love the moment of understanding
when an idea lands.

<div style="background: var(--callout-color); padding-inline: var(--gap);">

I prefer a _pay-what-you-want_ (plus shipping) model,
but include suggested prices
for people who get stressed about open-ended questions.
If you like a piece,
you should have it.
And if you can support my effort,
that means the world to me.
