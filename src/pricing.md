---
title: Pricing art sucks
sub: If you like it, you should have it
---

There is no reasonable way
to price handmade artwork.

1. I enjoy pouring my
   care and attention into these objects,
   and have no desire to
   'optimize' that process
   for maximum profit.
   I want you to feel my care and attention
   every time you interact with one of my pieces.
2. I want you to have art in your life,
   even if you aren't flush with cash.
   If you like what I make,
   I want it to be affordable to you.
   Not _cheap_, but _within reach_.
3. I need to pay for clay and glaze,
   food, tools, housing, and so on.

I post prices for each piece,
estimating the range where I think they belong.
But all prices are suggested,
and you should pay what feels viable to you
(and also cover shipping).

No need to negotiate,
just _pay what you can_.

If you're able to pay
more than the suggestion,
that helps cover people
who can't afford as much --
and it also helps me to keep making.
Thank you!
